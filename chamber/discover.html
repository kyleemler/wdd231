Why Broken Arrow Works

Broken Arrow is one of Oklahoma's largest cities, with a 2020 Census population of 113,540. Census QuickFacts reports 2.65 persons per household and a 2020-2024 median household income of $86,765, giving the community a strong residential base for local employers and member businesses.
